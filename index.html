How it works

  1. Build the room once. Tables, seats, the board, doors and windows. Every new chart starts from the same room.
  2. Add your class. Paste names from a spreadsheet or your school’s system, or import from Google Classroom.
  3. Set your rules. “Keep Liam and Owen apart.” Front row, same group, spread out a tag. Type them in plain words if you like.
  4. Seat everyone in one click. Every rule kept, groups mixed, no repeat partners. Print it or put it on the projector.

Everything a seating chart should do

⇄

Rules that stick

Keep two students apart, seat a student in the front row, or put two in the same group, or just type it: “Keep Liam and Owen apart.” Rules belong to the class, so every new chart follows them.

▦

Tables and groups

Tables of any size, split into pairs, trios or solo seats. Draw your real room, any shape, or let ClassMap arrange it for you.

⚖︎

Fair mixing

Mix boys and girls (or any groups you define) in every pair and table. Tag students like “IEP” or “Strong reader” to spread them out or pair them with a helper.

↻

New partners every time

ClassMap remembers your last charts and avoids seating the same students together again, without breaking any of your rules.

🎲

Tools for every day

Attendance on the chart, participation points, a name picker that gives everyone a turn, a group maker, a timer, and name tents to print.

▶

Print, present, hand off

Print a clean chart, or a substitute version with your notes and rules. Put it on the projector so students find their seats. Works in English and Spanish.

Your class lists never leave your device.
