Seating charts

Seating charts that follow your rules.

  1. Build the room once. Tables, seats, the board, doors and windows. Every new chart starts from the same room.
  2. Add your class. Paste names from a spreadsheet or your school’s system, or import from Google Classroom.
  3. Set your rules. “Keep Liam and Owen apart.” Front row, same group, spread out a tag. Type them in plain words if you like.
  4. Seat everyone in one click. Every rule kept, groups mixed, no repeat partners. Print it or put it on the projector.
