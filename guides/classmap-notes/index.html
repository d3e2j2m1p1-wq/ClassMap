ClassMap Notes: a guide for teachers

Planning notes and a class whiteboard in one place. Free, no account, and everything stays on your device.

What it is

ClassMap Notes is a notebook that runs in your browser. Each page can be a document (headings, checklists, tables, kanban boards, maths, YouTube videos) or a whiteboard you can draw on with a pen, a finger or a mouse. Open it at classmap.dev/notes.

Where your notes are kept

  • Only on this device, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account to make.
  • It works offline after your first visit, so a weak school Wi-Fi doesn't stop you.
  • Another device or browser starts empty. To move your notes, back them up on one and restore them on the other (see below).

Getting started

  • New page: press + next to Search in the sidebar.
  • Add things to a page: type / and pick a heading, checklist, table, kanban board, equation or YouTube video.
  • Whiteboard: use the switch at the top left of a page (it's called Edgeless). You get an endless board with a pen, shapes, sticky notes, mind maps and frames you can present as slides.
  • Journals give you a page for each day, handy for quick lesson notes.
  • Organise with folders, tags and favourites in the sidebar.

Templates

Your notebook starts with a Templates folder: a lesson plan, a weekly planner, a sub plan and a KWL chart. To start a page from one, choose Template in the sidebar. You can edit them, or turn any page of your own into a template.

Sub plans

The sub plan puts what matters most at the top, then the schedule, the plans for each period, procedures and emergencies. When it's ready, print it from the ••• menu (Export → Print). Print a fresh seating chart from ClassMap with Print → For a substitute and put it on top. Our substitute seating chart guide has more tips.

Back up your notes

Because notes live only in your browser, clearing its data or losing the device would lose them. Every two weeks ClassMap Notes reminds you to save a copy.

  • Back up: Settings → Storage → Back up everything. You get one .zip file in your Downloads.
  • Restore: Settings → Storage → Restore a backup. It adds the pages back and deletes nothing. Folders aren't included in a backup.

On an iPad

Open classmap.dev/notes in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen to get an app icon. The whiteboard works with an Apple Pencil.

Privacy

Student information you type stays on your device. Printouts are a different matter: keep sub plans to first names and what the sub needs, and leave medical or IEP details with the office.
